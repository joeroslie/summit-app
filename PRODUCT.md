# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Roofing company owners, salespeople, and estimators at companies that will buy Summit later. Joe is the first user and the creator. He and his crew are the proving ground; other companies come after.

## Product Purpose

Run the entire roofing job in one place: first lead through measure, price, order, labor, documents, calendar, and close.

Success is a roofing company finishing that job without bouncing between a CRM, a measuring tool, a spreadsheet, and a supplier portal.

## Positioning

A roofing operating system, not a generic CRM. Measure, locked company pricing, takeoff, orders, and field tools live with the job. A neighboring CRM could not truthfully claim that.

## Operating Context

People use Summit on a phone, iPad, laptop, or desktop — in the field and in the office. Phones can Add to Home Screen. A public webpage and an App Store listing are planned, not current.

The job runs through Home, Pipeline (jobs board), Estimates (measure and price), Takeoff, Orders and labor, Documents, Calendar, Tasks, Performance, Canvassing (door-knocking), Weather (storms), Company Pricing, and Settings.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Login for Joe is live. Extra-company signup, Apple login, billing, and invite screens are not built yet.
- Never invent prices or coverage numbers. Leave blanks until Joe confirms.
- Cash/insurance contracts, the sales pitch deck, and canvassing phone lookup are planned, not built.
- the estimator, invoices, and orders hang off a lead; Performance sits on Pipeline; Tools holds Canvassing and Weather rather than the main sidebar.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: **Summit**. App title also uses “Summit Roofing OS.”
- Voice: clear titles and actions. No filler lines under a title that only restate what the screen already is.
- Do not invent customer quotes, testimonials, or proof Joe did not supply.

## Evidence on Hand

- The running app itself (Home through Settings, plus Tools).
- Joe’s company price book — confirm with him; do not fill gaps.
- Visual explorations in `design-mockups/`. Warm Trade (cozy linen / terracotta) is rejected. A later color direction may still change; this file does not lock it.

No press, case studies, or third-party testimonials on hand. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. The job is the unit of work — every tool hangs off the job, not a separate silo.
2. Joe is first; other companies must still be able to run their own company later.
3. Money is exact: no invented prices.
4. Field-first: phone, tablet, and desk must all work.
5. Copy earns its place — no restating the screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Pinch-zoom stays on (field use and WCAG 1.4.4). On touch devices, text fields stay at least 16px so the phone does not zoom into focus, and header/sidebar controls are at least 44px.
